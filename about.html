Our Story

A quiet place to slow down.

Ongersfontein Guest Farm is a peaceful Karoo destination where travellers can step away from the rush and enjoy the simple beauty of farm life.

Set on a working cattle farm near Richmond in the Northern Cape, Ongersfontein combines comfortable accommodation with the character and tranquillity of the Karoo.

Our five cottages offer private entrances, cosy living areas, fully equipped kitchens and free WiFi. Outside, guests can enjoy lush gardens, braai areas and the wide-open atmosphere that makes the Karoo so special.
