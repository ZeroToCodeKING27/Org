The Karoo Way

Less rushing. More noticing.

Ongersfontein is a place to slow your pace and appreciate the character of the Karoo. Wide-open surroundings, changing light and quiet moments are all part of the experience.

Whether you are stopping over on your journey or staying a little longer, there is something special about having room to breathe.

A Different Pace

Come for the stay. Remember the feeling.

The beauty of a Karoo farm stay isn't always about filling every hour. Sometimes it is about waking up somewhere peaceful, spending time outdoors and enjoying a landscape that asks nothing from you.

Ongersfontein gives you the opportunity to experience that slower rhythm for yourself.
